# Security Policy

Thanks for helping keep SudarshanTechLabs projects and their users safe.

## Reporting a Vulnerability

**Please report security issues privately — do not open a public issue or pull request.**

Preferred: use GitHub's **private vulnerability reporting** — open the affected
repository's **Security** tab and click **Report a vulnerability**.

Alternatively, email **sudarshantechlabs@gmail.com** with `SECURITY` in the subject.

Please include what you have:

- A description of the issue and its potential impact
- Steps to reproduce (proof-of-concept if possible)
- Affected version, package, or commit
- Any suggested remediation

## What to Expect

These are solo-maintained projects. I aim to:

- Acknowledge your report within **5 business days**
- Confirm the issue and determine its scope
- Work on a fix for confirmed, in-scope issues as promptly as is practical
- Keep you updated on progress

Please give a reasonable window for a fix before any public disclosure. I'm
happy to credit reporters who wish to be named.

## Scope

This policy is the default for all SudarshanTechLabs repositories that do not
provide their own `SECURITY.md`. Each project is provided as-is under the terms
of its own `LICENSE`. Reports are most valuable for issues affecting users of
the published apps, packages, and tools — for example: credential/secret
exposure, injection, authentication or authorization flaws, and supply-chain
risks in released artifacts.
